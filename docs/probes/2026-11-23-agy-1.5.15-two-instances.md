# agy 1.5.15: two instances side by side in one repo (2026-11-23)

The probe for `docs/backlog/same-harness-pair.md`: can an agy driver and an agy navigator share a
repo and a session dir? Two interactive TUIs ran in agterm, started within a second of each other
from the same clone of tandem. They were named alpha and bravo; bravo had `--model
gemini-3.8-flash-low`. Each was launched as:

```
cd <clone> && TANDEM_ROLE=<r> agy --settings <profile> [--model …] --log-file <log> \
  --add-dir <shared> --add-dir <r> --prompt-interactive "<prompt>"
```

`<shared>` stood in for the session dir and had no `.agents/`. `<r>` held
`.agents/rules/AGENTS.md`, which gave that instance its own marker (`ALPHA-1` or `BRAVO-1`), and
`.agents/hooks.json`, whose SessionStart, PreInvocation, Stop and PreToolUse hooks logged their
dir, `TANDEM_ROLE`, the payload's `conversationId` and their parent pid. The profile allowed only
the probe's own commands.

## Isolation holds

- **Separate processes, separate language servers.** Each agy listened on its own loopback ports
  (`localhost:58464` and `localhost:58466`), had its own `ANTIGRAVITY_LS_ADDRESS` and CSRF token in
  `run_command` children, and held exactly one presence lock, for its own conversation.
- **Each loaded only its own `.agents/`, from the second `--add-dir`.** Alpha answered `ALPHA-1`
  and bravo `BRAVO-1`. agy's log said `loaded 1 named hooks from 1 hooks.json file(s)`, and every
  hook that fired came from the instance's own dir. A customizations dir doesn't have to be the
  first `--add-dir`, and a dir without `.agents/` loads nothing.
- **Hooks inherit the launcher's environment**: `TANDEM_ROLE` was right in every hook of a turn
  that ran in its own process. They still get no `ANTIGRAVITY_LS_ADDRESS`, as in 1.5.4.
- **`--model` is per instance.** Bravo's banner and its model calls used Gemini 3.8 Flash (Low),
  and alpha kept the default.
- **The working directory is the last `--add-dir`**: `pwd` in a `run_command` printed the role
  dir, not the repo and not `<shared>`. With one `--add-dir`, the session dir, this matches the
  2026-11-12 finding that agy runs commands in the session dir.

## Follow-up in print mode: nesting and order

The fix nests each role's `.agents/` under the session dir, as `<s>/peers/<role>/.agents/`, so
two more questions were checked with `agy -p`:

- `--add-dir <s>` alone, with `.agents/` only in `<s>/peers/driver/` and `<s>/peers/navigator/`:
  the model answered `NONE`. A nested `.agents/` isn't discovered.
- `--add-dir <s>/peers/driver --add-dir <s>`: `pwd` printed `<s>`, and the driver's marker
  loaded. The working directory follows the order of the `--add-dir`s, and customizations load
  from any of them, the first included.

## Rules are re-read every turn

After alpha's first answer, its `AGENTS.md` was edited to say `ALPHA-2`. Asked again in a new
turn, alpha answered `ALPHA-2`, with no relaunch and no `/new`. One `.agents/rules/AGENTS.md`
shared by two agys would therefore give the first one the second launch's protocol on its next
turn.

## A ring to the wrong conversation runs it in the wrong process

With alpha's LS address and token, `agy agentapi send-message <alpha's conversation>` woke alpha,
which answered within seconds; bravo's screen didn't change. Then the same LS was given **bravo's**
conversation id:

- agentapi returned success, and neither TUI showed anything.
- Alpha's process ran the turn: its log has `SendAgentMessage` followed by a model call to bravo's
  model, `gemini-3.8-flash`. Bravo's log has nothing.
- The turn fired **bravo's** hooks (PreInvocation and Stop, from bravo's dir) as children of
  **alpha's** pid, with `TANDEM_ROLE=alpha` and bravo's conversation id. The conversation carries
  its workspace dirs, so the process that runs it loads that conversation's customizations under
  its own environment.
- Asked afterwards whether any message mentioned the ring's text, bravo answered `NONE`. The turn
  stayed in alpha's process: a lost doorbell, not a message bravo receives later.

## What it means for an agy + agy pair

- Feasible: nothing collides at the process, LS or presence-lock level.
- `.agents/` has to move from the session dir root to a per-role dir passed as a second
  `--add-dir`. The session dir itself must not keep an `.agents/`, or both agys load it.
- `peers/agy/conversation_id` has to be per role. A watcher that rings its own LS with the other
  agy's conversation loses the ring, and the hooks of that stray turn would record the wrong
  conversation under the ringing role. The presence lock found by agy's pid stays the primary
  source.
- Passing the role dir first and the session dir last keeps agy's commands starting in the session
  dir, as the protocol says.
